In the 1990s, a programming skill had a half-life of 10-15 years. By 2020, it was 5 years. In 2026, the half-life of specialized syntax is measured in months.

How do you build a career when the technical foundations are shifting under your feet? You focus on **non-depreciating skills**.

## Non-Depreciating Skills

These are skills that retain value regardless of what models are released:
*   **System Architecture**: Knowing how to structure databases, design APIs, and connect services.
*   **Problem Formulation**: The ability to break down vague user requirements into explicit specifications.
*   **Deep Domain Knowledge**: Understanding the actual mechanics of a business or sector (e.g., finance, biology, design).
