As AI models become cheaper and faster, we are entering a world of hyper-abundance. Code, writing, and design assets can be generated in milliseconds for fraction-of-a-cent costs.

In this environment of abundance, **execution speed is no longer a differentiator**. The ultimate moat is **judgment and taste**.

## The Commodity of Synthesis

If anyone can generate a 1,000-word marketing email or a landing page with a single prompt, the internet will be flooded with mediocre synthesis. 

The value shifts from *creation* to *curation*. It is the human gatekeeper who says: *"This is beautiful, this is aligned, and this solves the actual user problem. That other generated option is noise."*

## Cultivating Your Taste Moat

1.  **Expose Yourself to High-Quality Inputs**: Read timeless classics, inspect award-winning designs, and study robust system architectures.
2.  **Develop a Critical Eye**: Don't accept AI outputs at face value. Edit them, question their assumptions, and refine them until they feel premium.
